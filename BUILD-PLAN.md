# Racing for Jesus — The Great Commission Road
### a co-op Bible driving-and-mission adventure for the whole family (2–8 players, Xbox/TV + phone)
**© 2026 Taiwo Adegoke · All rights reserved · KJV (public domain)**

> *"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations… and, lo, I am with you alway." (Matthew 28:19–20)*

A new standalone single-file HTML5 **canvas** game (vanilla JS, 960×540 internal letterboxed, one rAF loop, WebAudio, **no external assets**, offline PWA) — the same engine ethos as the David platformer and the Jonah adventure, and it **extends the proven couch-co-op plumbing in** `C:\Users\adego\Documents\BibleAdventureGame\david-coop.html` (player slots, pad loop, colour-coded prompts). Designed by a 6-agent workshop (5 design pillars → 50 scenes → a guardrail audit, run `wf_bd2d4aad-943`).

## 1. The one idea — you carry the WORD, and the LIGHT grows
You and your family are the child **labourers Jesus SENT** (*"as my Father hath sent me, even so send I you,"* John 20:21). You **drive out, find Bibles, carry them the long or short road, give them away, hug and comfort people with the right Word, and stop to pray** — and as you do, a **grey, colour-drained world warms to full colour, lamps light, and the gloom lifts and flees.** You **never win by fighting.** The whole 50-scene map expands **outward along Acts 1:8** — *Jerusalem → Judaea → Samaria → the uttermost part of the earth* — so every new region is the gospel reaching farther, and the Pastor's **"Word-as-seed → fruit"** (Psalm 126:6) becomes the literal end-game harvest.

## 2. The two meters — theologically true, kept child-safe
The Pastor's *"Kingdom of God bar up / kingdom of Satan bar down"* is built as **LIGHT growing and DARKNESS receding — and nothing else.** **The enemy is NEVER embodied, named, or shown** — no devil, demon, monster, face, horns, or eyes. The down-meter is labelled **"the darkness" / "the gloom,"** only ever a fog or shadow that peels back.
- **Kingdom-of-God meter (rises):** Matthew 6:10 · Daniel 2:44; 7:14 · Luke 17:21 · Revelation 11:15 — a grey town warms to colour, lamps light, flowers open.
- **Darkness meter (falls):** Colossians 1:13 · John 1:5 · 1 John 2:8 · Isaiah 9:2 — a grey fog simply lifts and flees.

Both move on the **same loving acts**: a Bible delivered, a person hugged + given the right Word, a prayer completed. **One shared pair of meters persists across all 50 scenes**, so progress is continuous.

## 3. The core loop & verbs
**Drive → choose a fork (long/short road) → evade or shield a trap → dock at a face → HUG → pick the right Word → the Light rises → drive on.** The gospel verb set is always **FIND → CARRY → GIVE → HUG & COMFORT → PRAY → the Light grows.**

| Verb | Input (gamepad / key / touch) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Drive / steer | stick · D-pad · arrows · touch-drag | move the vehicle; off-road just slows you |
| Deliver a Bible | Ⓐ at a person-node | +1 person reached; meters move |
| Hug → Word | Ⓐ to hug, then stick+Ⓐ to choose the Word | the right KJV verse rises as a gift (§6) |
| Plead the Blood | Ⓑ in the timed window | radiant shield; pass the gloom safely (§8) |
| Pray (Prayer Station) | hold Ⓨ | a Selah prayer-stop; refills the day (§7) |
| Load / swap vehicle | Ⓐ at a Depot / Truck Depot | capacity gating (§5) |

## 4. The "timeframe" — a forgiving DAY that PRAYER refills (the key systems idea)
A **Sun Arc** drifts dawn→dusk across the top = **150 s of *active driving* per region** (it **pauses during hugs, prayer, menus, and the Plead prompt** — thinking is never penalised). **Reaching sunset never fails you** — the sky warms to evening, the sun becomes a gentle moon, and a kind line shows: *"Evening came — keep going, there's grace for the journey."* You only miss an optional daylight bonus. **And every Prayer Station tops the Sun Arc back up (+40 s; the mandatory Daniel gates fully refill it)** — so the game teaches *"when you're running low, STOP AND PRAY — prayer gives you strength to go on."* **No lives, no death, no Game Over.**

## 5. The vehicle ladder (monotonic — unlocked once, never re-unlocked)
**Car (3 Bibles) → Van (6) → Gospel Truck (12) → the Good News Ship → the Convoy/fleet.** When `remaining_people > capacity`, a friendly Truck-Depot prompt glows (*"So many to reach! Bring the big truck?"*) — but you can **always** do it the slow way with the small car; the bigger vehicle just saves trips and day-meter. In co-op the vehicle also scales **seats** to the player count (§9).

## 7. Prayer Stations + the mandatory "pray like Daniel" gates
Anchor: **Daniel 6:10** — *he kneeled… three times a day, and prayed, and gave thanks.* A Prayer Station **reuses Selah's Rest flow exactly**: a real KJV verse pinned on top (gold, distinct), a short prayer/adoration line (plain) the child dwells in over one slow breathing ring (~5 s), then *"Amen → continue."* **You cannot fail a prayer**; the world sits in a calm paused "Selah" state. Reward: **Sun Arc +40 s, +8 Light, nearby gloom clears.**

**FIVE MANDATORY hard-gate prayer stops** (you cannot drive through until the party prays; AI fills empty seats so a solo 4-year-old is never stuck), spaced morning/noon/evening across the journey: **Scene 5** (Daniel's Morning Prayer — the keystone), **Scene 15** (the Noonday Altar), **Scene 25** (Pray the Lord of the Harvest — spawns labourer NPCs), **Scene 39** (the Fourth Watch — the storm-deck kneel), **Scene 44** (Evening Prayer — whole-party). Plus optional stations (sc7, 18, 22 the Jabez enlarge-prayer, 28) and prayer-powered climaxes (sc10, 36).

## 8. "Plead the Blood of Jesus" — the shield (no combat, no gore)
A snare approaches → a bright prompt pops **"PLEAD THE BLOOD OF JESUS ✦"** → press **Ⓑ** in time → a **radiant GOLD/white shield bubble** blooms and you pass as the shadows flee (+2 Light, a *"COVERED! / SAFE IN JESUS!"* chime). **Miss it → child-safe grace:** the car slows ~1.2 s in dimmer fog, then an **auto-shield ALWAYS fires** (*"Jesus covers us — let's keep going."*). So it *teaches the reflex* but a child who never presses still always gets through. Anchors (taught reverently): Exodus 12:13 · Revelation 12:11 · Hebrews 12:24 · 1 John 1:7. Traps are only **snares & weights** (Psalm 91:3; Hebrews 12:1) — potholes, mud, fog, closed roads — **evaded by steering, never destroyed, never a creature.**

## 9. Co-op & Xbox/TV (first-class) — ONE shared vehicle, many stations
**All players ride ONE shared vehicle on one road, one camera — never split-screen.** Each player mans a **STATION (a job)** so up to 8 kids share without chaos and the youngest is never stranded. Seats/cargo/meters persist across all 50 scenes.

**Roles (each = one clear button in your own colour):** Driver (steer/evade) · Navigator (reads the map, picks the long/short fork, drops the "go here" marker) · Loader (loads/hands out Bibles — cargo cap is why you need the Truck) · **Hugger** (gives the hug → the Word — a low-skill/high-joy seat a 4-year-old owns) · **Watchman** (Pleads the Blood, calls & leads the Prayer Station) · Lookout (6p) · Light-Bearer (7p, pumps the Light meter with praise) · Roaming Helper (8p). A 10-second **"Switch Seats?"** board appears at each rest-stop (default = keep your seat).

**Scaling:** 1 solo (Car, AI covers all other seats — a **complete** game) · 2 (Car) · 3–4 (Van) · 5–8 (Truck). **Gamepad (Xbox):** left stick/D-pad steer, RT accel, LT brake, Ⓐ context/deliver/hug/pray, Ⓑ plead the Blood, Ⓧ watchman, Ⓨ request seat-swap; a 2nd+ controller joins by any face button; **connecting a gamepad forces TV Mode** (big text, point-and-A menus for a 720p panel). **Keyboard + phone-touch** fully supported. **Build note:** `david-coop.html` caps players at 4 (`addPlayer`/`renderSlots`) — raise to 8 and extend `COLORS` to 8 high-contrast hues legible on both grey and colour towns.

## 10. Art direction — "graphics ON POINT" on procedural canvas
The signature is the **grey→colour BLOOM**: towns render in drained greyscale and **warm to full saturated colour** as the Light meter crosses thresholds (per-building, per-street, per-region, then the whole map at once in the finale). Push the canvas: layered gradients, radial glows, `globalCompositeOperation='lighter'` for lamplight/sun/shields, parallax road & scenery, weather & a real day→dusk→night sky, particles (sheaves of light, confetti, fog peeling back), screen-shake/juice. **HUD:** the two meters (Kingdom rising / darkness receding), the map of happy/sad faces, the Bible/cargo counter, the Souls-Reached tally, and the Sun Arc timer. Keep the **animation-state seam** so hand-drawn sprites can drop in later. Child-safe cartoon warmth throughout.

## 11. THE GUARDRAIL (absolute) + the audit's locked fixes
Real KJV **only**, in gold Cinzel + ✦ + exact reference — the *only* thing ever styled that way. All invented mission framing (towns, Bibles, cargo, the Light, the shield, the "darkness," the verbs) is plain/italic, **never** a collectible or prompt dressed as Scripture. The two-meter "darkness" is **never** an embodied or named Satan. Child-safe ages ~4–8: no gore, no death, nothing that frightens. The audit **APPROVED** the design and all 50 KJV references checked out as real and correctly numbered. **Lock these 7 fixes into the build:**

- **Scene 1 — Jesus is present as 'a soft radiant Light with a kind voice' that 'sends you' (also scenes 32 'hands laid on', 41 'the Jesus-light speaks the Sending', 49).** → Hard rule: the Jesus-Light may 'speak' ONLY verbatim cited KJV, always rendered gold Cinzel + ✦ + exact reference. Every other line (encouragement, instructions, 'well done') comes from an unattributed narrator/guide voice in plain Inter and is NEVER framed as God speaking. Add an engine assert that any text routed to the Light actor must carry a real kjv.json reference.
- **Scene 36 — The gospel-answer the player gives the jailer is called 'the plain gospel answer card (clear "believe on the Lord Jesus")' in the mechanics, but it IS Acts 16:31 verbatim and is shown gold ✦ in the scriptureMoment.** → Split the two layers: the child's selectable button is a plain paraphrase ('Tell him about Jesus' / 'Welcome them in') in Inter and is never a verse; on commit, the real KJV (Acts 16:31 etc.) then rises DISTINCT in gold ✦ with its reference as the gift. A verse is never a button that could be 'picked wrong'.
- **Scene 44 — The mandatory-Daniel count is internally contradictory.** → Adopt ONE canonical frame: a Daniel prayer anchors each act, and across the journey's days the children pray again and again 'as Daniel did three times a day.' Never label any single gate 'the last/third of three.' Correct the roster to the five real gates (sc5 morning, sc15 noon, sc25 noon, sc39 night-watch, sc44 evening). This exceeds the required 3 and removes the contradiction.
- **Scene 38 — The Euroclydon storm is the highest fright risk in the game for a 4-year-old: pre-dawn dark, racing clouds, rain-streaks, a pitching/rotating hull, AND a 'fear meter on the crew' with frightened faces.** → Show Paul's 'be of good cheer: there shall be no loss of any man's life' BEFORE any weather ramps, and keep it on-screen as a safety promise throughout. No thunderclaps; render crew worry as mild mood-badges, not distressed faces. Default the calm-sea + reduced-motion assist ON for the Little-Ones profile (lowest pitch/shake), and keep the 'storm visibly abating as courage rises' feedback fast so tension only ever drops.
- **Scene 4 — The Pastor's brief names a 'Kingdom of the devil/Satan' bar going down.** → Keep the guardrail-driven choice and make it explicit: the down-meter is labelled 'the darkness' (or 'the gloom'), never 'Satan,' and is only ever shown as fog/shadow receding — no face, eyes, horns, or figure anywhere in the game. This honours the brief's two-meter mechanic while staying age-safe.
- **Scene 6 — 'Plead the Blood' uses a 'crimson-gold' shield (also scenes 24, 46).** → Constrain the palette: the shield is GOLD/white-dominant with crimson only as a thin warm rim or inner glow — never a red splash or spatter. Keep the 'PLEAD THE BLOOD OF JESUS' caption as plain UI instruction (already done). Reads as a bubble of Jesus' love and light, never blood.
- **Scene 31 — The 'SHINE to turn a wrong-way traveller around' beat involves a car driving the wrong way toward you; if staged tightly it can read as a near-collision / game-of-chicken, which undercuts the non-violent, non-scary intent.** → Enforce generous spacing and a gentle, telegraphed U-turn arc well before the vehicles close; the beam 'invites' the turn, never forces a swerve. Add an explicit no-near-miss rule (minimum gap) so the moment always reads as kindness turning a heart, not a dodge.

*KJV note:* All KJV references across all 50 scenes were checked and are real, correctly numbered, public-domain KJV verses, aptly matched to the feeling/theme (e.g. Psalms 34:18 for broken-hearted, Isaiah 41:10 for afraid, Genesis 50:20 for betrayed, Jeremiah 29:11 for forgotten, Ephesians 4:32 for forgiveness, Acts 16:14 for Lydia's opened heart, Acts 27:35 + Psalms 107:29 for the stilled storm, Philippians 3:14 for the finishing race). No fabricated or nonexistent reference was found, and chapter/verse numbers are valid for their books (incl. 1 Chronicles 4:9-10 Jabez, Psalms 126 having 6 verses, Daniel 3:25 'like the Son of God'). ONE non-KJV error to fix: scene 44's prose cites the morning Daniel gate as 'sc.10' — it is scene 5 (scene 10 is the Lions' Court). That is a scene-number citation error, not a Scripture error. Reminder: because the exact text is pulled from kjv.json at build, verify the build resolves every reference against the existing kjv.json (present in src\, www\, and both native asset copies) and fails loudly on any miss.

## 13. Build order (vertical slice first, then outward — like Jonah)
The audit's recommended first slice proves the whole pipeline on the smallest set:
1. **Scaffold** the standalone single-file shell (reuse `david-coop.html` co-op plumbing + the Jonah `caption()` Scripture guardrail + Selah Rest flow for prayer + `kjv.json` resolver with load-time asserts).
2. **Core systems:** road/drive + camera, the Depot→deliver loop, the **two meters + grey→colour bloom**, the **Sun-Arc day-meter that prayer refills**, the vehicle-capacity swap.
3. **Vertical slice — Scenes 1, 3, 4, 5, 6** (Drive & Deliver → Hug→Word → the two meters → the first Daniel Prayer gate → Plead the Blood). ← *first playable slice to show Pastor.*
4. Finish **Act I (1–10)**, then build **outward act by act** (Abraham/Joseph → Jabez/Harvest → Paul → the Great Commission finale), verifying each in the browser pane at 1280×720 (TV size).
5. **Co-op pass** (8 seats, roles, TV Mode), **touch pass**, **meta** (world map, Scripture Gallery), then a **guardrail + safety + performance audit.**

**Tech:** single-file, dependency-free, offline PWA; KJV pulled from the existing `src/kjv.json`; prayers from the Selah bank (`selah-themes.json`/`selah-attrs.json`). Standalone own app — suggested repo `tboy2020/racing-for-jesus`. **Deploy only when Pastor asks directly.**
