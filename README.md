# Racing for Jesus

A co-op Bible **driving-and-mission adventure** for the whole family — carry the Word to
the world. Deliver Bibles, bring the big Gospel Truck, let the light grow and the darkness
recede, and stop to pray along the way. Built as a single self-contained `index.html` — no
build step, no dependencies, runs offline on a phone, tablet, or TV.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys / WASD to drive · **F** / Space for the context action (deliver,
  load, pray, swap to the truck) · **Enter** to advance screens.
- **Touch:** on-screen D-pad + action button (phones & tablets).
- **Couch co-op (up to 8 players):** plug in game controllers and press a button to join —
  the game switches to **TV Mode** with a colour-coded roster. One shared vehicle: a single
  **Driver** steers; everyone else rides along and helps with deliveries and prayer. Younger
  players are never stuck — a gentle AI quietly fills any empty or idle seat and steps back
  the moment someone takes over.

## The journey

A continuous adventure across Acts:

- **Act I — Daniel's City of Prayer** (scenes 1–10)
- **Act II — Abraham's Road & Joseph's Egypt** (scenes 11–20)

…with more Acts on the road ahead.

## Scripture

All Scripture shown is the **King James Version (KJV)**, public domain, displayed distinctly
with its exact reference. Invented narration and gameplay text are kept visually separate and
are never presented as the words of God.
